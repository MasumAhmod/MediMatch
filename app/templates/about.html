About MediMatch

AI-powered disease prediction and specialist recommendation to help you find the right medical care.

What is MediMatch?

MediMatch is an AI-powered healthcare assistance system designed to help users understand their symptoms and find an appropriate medical specialist.

Users can enter their symptoms and receive a predicted disease from our machine learning model. Based on the predicted disease, MediMatch recommends the appropriate medical specialization.

Users can then find doctors who belong to that specialization and explore their professional information.
